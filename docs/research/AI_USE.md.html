AI use in this study

This file states who did what. AI systems appear in two different roles here: as subjects of the experiment (the drafter and judge being evaluated) and as research assistants (tools that helped the author build, run and document the study). The two roles are kept separate.

Limitations from AI involvement

  • Mixed AI authorship; tested models from one family. Materials, references and documentation were prepared with both Claude and OpenAI assistants, while both tested models, the drafter and the judge, are Claude models. Shared assumptions or blind spots could favor or disadvantage particular answers. Independent human reviewers and judges from other providers are needed to test this.
  • Non-blind, AI-assisted review. The author's adjudication was non-blind and conducted through discussion with Codex. The final rulings are the author's, but the framing of each issue was shaped by that discussion.
  • AI-polished documentation. The publication text was edited and polished with AI assistance from the author's content and drafts. Editing may have introduced errors the author did not catch.

None of the AI assistance above constitutes independent attorney, expert or peer review.
