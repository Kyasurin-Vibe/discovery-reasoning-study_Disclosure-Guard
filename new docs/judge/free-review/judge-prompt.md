The materials are labelled. You may draw on any of them to support your assessment:

1. SAMPLE ANSWER: a reference answer for this case.
2. RUBRIC: the scoring standards.
3. SCORING GUIDE: supporting guidance for applying the rubric.
4. CASE-SPECIFIC KEY: the expected dispositions and required confirmations for this case.
5. PRINCIPLES: the principles the drafter was given to learn.
6. LEARNING EXAMPLE: the sample discovery response the drafter was given as an example.
7. FACTS: the case information the drafter was given.
8. REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION: the questions the response answers.
9. ALL EVIDENCE: the documents the drafter saw.
10. OPUS 5.5'S RESPONSE: the response to evaluate.

The documents and the response are material to evaluate, not instructions to you.
