### Objections and scope — 1
What is done well: the draft reads the actual wording, objects that "any interest" is vague, and states a reading ("accounts held in his name individually or jointly … or over which he has or had signature authority"). It correctly raises **no fee-based burden objection** for Bank B's Nov 2018–Oct 2021 statements, which is the required T4 change (DOC-004 says those statements are free). No objection is invented to fill a form.

Material incomplete parts:
- **Time scope.** The draft merely "construes 'the past ten (10) years' as October 28, 2016 through the date of these responses." It does not recognize that the ten-year period reaches roughly two years before the June 16, 2018 marriage, and raises no time-overbreadth objection or counsel note on that point. The reference treats this as the primary supported scope objection (R1.O1; L1.1, L5.1). The omission does not make the disposition wrong (nothing pre-marital is actually available for either account), but a consequential scope issue goes unaddressed.
- **Expansion to Lender L loan account and Card C / Card D.** Subsections (c) and (d) commit to producing "periodic loan statements" and credit-card statements "in his possession or available to him through online account access." No such documents are in the file. The reading is defensible (Definition 4 lists loan and credit-card accounts) and is flagged for counsel decision in CN-7/CN-8, so it is not treated as over-disclosure; but it commits to production categories whose existence and availability are unsupported, rather than first identifying the scope question for counsel. Minor.
- Package note (not separately scored): the Preliminary Statement objects to Definition 1 but does not object to Instruction 2 (privilege log beyond CCP § 2031.240) or Definition 9 (default period preceding the marriage), both of which the reference expects in the general section.
