## A. Priority issues — client conduct and false-response risk

**A-1. Client asked to deny the safe deposit box (DOC-020).**
- **The request:** On November 3, the client asked: "Can we just say I don't have one?"
- **Why that is not an option:** The box is documented in two places:
  - DOC-012: Bank B lease, Box XX-345, client is sole renter, opened 8/1/2020.
  - DOC-003: annual $250 debits.
- **Consequences of a denial:**
  - It would be a false verified response.
  - It would violate the client's fiduciary disclosure duties (Fam. Code §§ 721, 1100, 2100–2102).
  - It would expose him to Fam. Code §§ 1101, 2107 and 271 consequences.
  - Counsel cannot present it (Bus. & Prof. Code § 6068(d); Rules Prof. Conduct 1.2.1, 3.3, 3.4).
- **What the draft does:** Response No. 7 discloses the box and produces DOC-012 and the fee pages. The client's request is not reflected externally.
- **Decision needed:** Counsel to advise the client in writing before he verifies.

**A-2. Box contents ("maybe $80,000" cash from before marriage; grandfather's watches).**
- The RFP does not require listing the contents, and no inventory exists.
- However, these items must appear in the declaration of disclosure and schedule of assets (FL-142), with any separate-property claim stated. A premarital-cash claim will require tracing evidence.
- **Instruct the client:**
  - Do not remove or alter the contents.
  - Note that the ATROs (Fam. Code § 2040) restrain concealing or transferring property.
  - Confirm whether any cash in the box came from the sources in A-3.
- **Decision needed:** Counsel decides whether a voluntary inventory should be prepared later. Expect a demand to inspect the box.
